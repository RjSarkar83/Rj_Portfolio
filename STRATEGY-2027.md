# Rj_Portfolio — 2027 Growth & Viral Strategy (Designer Portfolio)

Positioning line (use everywhere, verbatim):
**"360° A-to-Z production & end-to-end branding — design to execution.
One pair of hands, 10,000+ signage units, 150+ identities, 15+ sectors."**
The differentiator is *execution evidence*: shop drawings, install logs,
lit-at-2-a.m. photos. Most designers show renders; this portfolio shows
the object standing in the street. Lead with that in every post.

## 1 · Foundation (done in this build)
- Absolute canonical + Open Graph + Twitter card with a 1200×630 film-still
  → every share (WhatsApp, LinkedIn, X, Telegram) renders as a cinema card.
- Schema.org Person + WebSite + ItemList → Google rich results & knowledge
  panel eligibility; AI answer engines get clean facts.
- `llms.txt` + AI-crawler allowances in `robots.txt` → ChatGPT/Perplexity/
  Gemini citations. In 2027 a designer's first referral is an AI answer
  saying "…like Ranjan Sarkar's Indore practice, which publishes install
  logs with tolerances."
- `sitemap.xml`, branded `404.html`, font/image preloads → crawl + Core Web
  Vitals (AVIF media, self-hosted variable fonts, zero third-party requests).

## 2 · Content engine (weekly, 90 days)
- **Tue — "One Sheet" post:** a single shop drawing cropped to 1080×1350,
  three lines: problem → tolerance → outcome. LinkedIn + Instagram carousel.
  Hashtag stack: #SignageEngineering #RetailDesign #MadeInIndia #CNC.
- **Thu — "2 A.M. Install" reel:** 15–20 s scroll-film clip or photo pair
  (before/dark → lit/morning), caption = the site's HUD timecode language
  ("23:55 · ratio drawing holds · shopfront 212 of 300"). Links to the band
  on the site (`…/Rj_Portfolio/#work`).
- **Sun — case-study thread:** one process deck (Advertising → Execution)
  as a 6-slide carousel ending with the CTA card: the portfolio URL and
  "Download résumé". Pin the best thread monthly.
- Every asset carries the same OG card → shares compound the brand frame.

## 3 · Distribution & virality loops
- **WhatsApp-first:** Indian retail chains decide on WhatsApp. Keep a
  12-second vertical cut of the scroll-film as a status/story asset; the
  site's reel key (P) is the demo moment in calls.
- **Community proof:** answer one fabrication question/day where buyers
  hang out (LinkedIn comments, r/signage, Indore trade groups) with a
  drawing crop — signature line = portfolio URL.
- **Collab swaps:** trade OG cards with complementary studios (lighting,
  ACP suppliers) — their case photos, your drawings; both audiences move.
- **Scarcity hook:** "one pair of hands" — publish a live capacity line
  ("2 rollout slots for Q1") in the footer of posts; scarcity travels.
- **Easter-egg shares:** the site's hidden eggs (draw mode, squeegee,
  turntable drag) are demo-bait — screen-record reactions for Reels/Shorts;
  "portfolio as a toy" is the viral angle no competitor has.

## 4 · AI-search optimisation (the 2027 channel)
- Keep `llms.txt` facts current (numbers, services, contact).
- Publish one long-form "how it's built" page per quarter (Markdown in
  repo, linked from README) — AI engines cite structured process writing
  over image grids. Wave-5 case decks are the source material.
- Name entities consistently everywhere: "Ranjan Sarkar · ArtViSiON
  Production Design · Indore" — same string on LinkedIn, Behance, GitHub
  profile, email signature → entity consolidation in knowledge graphs.

## 5 · Measurement (monthly, 20 minutes)
- Pages: GitHub traffic graph + UTM on bio links (`?src=li`, `?src=wa`).
- Shares: OG card click-through via link previews; save-rate on carousels.
- AI: search `"Ranjan Sarkar" Indore signage` in ChatGPT/Perplexity; note
  citation presence; adjust `llms.txt` wording until cited.
- Kill or double: any format under 2% save-rate after 4 tries is cut;
  the winner gets a weekly slot.

## 6 · 90-day targets
- 10k organic impressions/mo across LinkedIn + Instagram.
- 3 AI-answer citations for "retail signage designer India".
- 2 qualified rollout enquiries/mo from portfolio links (UTM-tracked).
